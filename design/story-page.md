# The story page — design

Settled 2026-10-01, before it is built. Changes later are made here, dated, with the reason.

## What it is

A plain-language front page for the people `opengeo test` is for: small-business owners and
founders who are technical and hands-on, and who have been told to add FAQ schema, put the
answer first, and repeat their keywords. It tells them what a controlled experiment found,
how sure it is, where it stops, and how to run the same experiment on their own page.

It becomes `docs/index.html`. The current findings page, written for researchers and
journalists, moves to `docs/findings.html`, one click away. Both are generated by
`build_findings.py` from `results/findings.json`; nothing on either is typed by hand.

## The rules it inherits

- **Every figure comes from the ledger.** Story prose lives in the ledger's `story` block
  with `{placeholders}`, exactly like the findings page, and the build checks it the same
  way: typed numbers are flagged, and sentences a reader would take as fact ("on every
  model") carry named assertions.
- **Only published results.** The real-page observational analysis and the real-text H4
  round are not published, so the page does not mention them.
- **It measures; it doesn't prescribe.** The line from `ROADMAP.md`: no checklist, no "you
  should". It relays what was found and how strongly, and ends with "test it on your page".
- **Direction, not size.** The +48 is reported as what happened in those tests. The vague
  pages answered nothing and the specific page was usually the only one that did, so
  against competitors that also answer, the gain will be smaller. The page says so where
  the number appears, not in a footnote.

## The worked example (section 2)

One of the 48 questions, `cons_shoes`, shown as tested: the question, the vague document,
the specific one. Its per-engine counts are stored in the ledger and recomputed from
`results/runs_v0.4.jsonl` at every build; the build fails if they differ. It is labelled
"one of the 48", with the all-question average beside it, because this question's effect is
above the average and showing it alone would oversell.

The answer shown is a real response from that round, quoted as the model wrote it, chosen
by run key in the ledger.

## Charts

Built to the dataviz skill's specs: thin marks, solid hairline grids (no dashed zero lines),
a 2px surface ring on dots, selective direct labels, hover and keyboard tooltips that never
gate a value, and a "the numbers" table under every chart. Colour does one job per chart:

- **Emphasis** for the effects scale: the accent for the one result that is the story, gray
  for the rest.
- **One hue, two steps** for before and after, validated as an ordinal pair in both modes.
- The findings page's green/red/gray for "interval clear of zero" stays on that page only.

Same type and surfaces as the findings page (Instrument Serif headings, Public Sans text,
IBM Plex Mono figures; the paper and ochre tokens), with hero figures in the sans.
