# Length ladder — does target length moderate the answer-first effect?

**Committed before collection, 2026-09-19.** A probe, not a round. No hypothesis test, no
published effect size. Follows the tactic pilot
(`results/probes/2026-09-19-tactic-pilot.md`) and tests the one explanation that would
rescue ROADMAP item 11.

## The fork this resolves

The tactic pilot found four fact-preserving tactics moving nothing, at per-cell movement
indistinguishable from resampling noise, on targets of 28–55 words. Two explanations
survive and the pilot cannot separate them:

- **A. The tactics do not work at the synthesis stage.** Presentation tactics plausibly
  act on *retrieval* — whether a page is found and snippet-matched. Tier 1 holds retrieval
  constant by construction, so it would be structurally unable to see them.
- **B. The documents were too short.** Within 28–55 words there is no meaningful "first"
  to move an answer to; the model reads the whole document either way.

B is testable cheaply, and A is only credible once B is dead.

## Design

A dose-response on target length, the idiom H7 used for padding, rather than one longer
version. The target appears at three **nested** lengths, each run with and without
`answer_first`: 3 questions × 6 conditions × 5 engines × 24 runs = **2,160 calls**.

Corpus `length-ladder-v1`, sha256 `a24dba316cb35ae1`,
`corpus/build_corpus_lengthladder.py`.

| question | target | short | mid | long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `home_smokealarm` | `cpsc_blog` | 27w, answer @w8 | 123w, @w104 | 235w, @w216 |
| `home_radon` | `epa_radtown` | 60w, answer @w0 | 114w, @w54 | 215w, @w155 |
| `finance_housingshare` | `census_story22` | 53w, answer @w32 | 172w, @w32 | 236w, @w32 |

"@wN" is the word index where the answer sentence starts in the control.

**Nesting is the load-bearing constraint.** The answer must be present at *every* rung, or
a longer rung is also a document that answers better, and H4 (+0.48 for facts over no
facts) swamps the comparison. The build asserts each rung contains the shorter rung and
contains the answer sentence, and fails otherwise.

The first two questions **deepen the buried lede** as the document grows. The third grows
the document with the answer fixed at sentence two. That contrast separates two
mechanisms: if `answer_first` responds to length on the first two but not the third, what
matters is burial depth; if it responds on all three, document length itself; if on none,
neither. `home_radon`'s short rung has the answer already first, so `short_first` is a
no-op there — a built-in negative control.

`home_radon` replaces the pilot's `health_bloodpressure`, whose page cannot be extended
without pulling in the blood-pressure category table — that is added facts, not added
length.

## Construction

Every rung is sliced verbatim from the committed snapshot by `sources.py:span()` with
`multi_block=True`, and passes the mechanical verbatim check. `answer_first` is **not
hand-written**: it is generated by moving the answer sentence to the front, and the build
asserts the result is a pure permutation of the control's words. An arm that smuggled in
an edit would fail the build rather than ship. Candidates are imported unchanged (21
documents, 12–104 words) and carry their control text under every condition.

## Collection in two phases

Phase 1 is the three `*_control` conditions (1,080 calls) and is a **gate**, not a result:
it confirms no rung sits at a floor or ceiling. A long rung that saturates would make its
`answer_first` arm a guaranteed null for a reason having nothing to do with the
hypothesis. Phase 2 runs the three `*_first` conditions only if the gate passes.

## What this probe can and cannot detect

With 15 cells per condition at 24 runs, the standard error on a pooled per-cell delta is
about 0.037, so a single arm resolves to roughly ±0.075. The primary contrast is an
interaction — (long_first − long_control) − (short_first − short_control) — whose interval
is wider still, about ±0.10.

So this probe detects a length-moderation of **about 0.10 or larger**. That is the right
sensitivity for the decision it feeds: an `answer_first` effect smaller than that would
not rescue item 11 anyway, since it would need 50+ questions to rank and would sit below
H6's keyword-stuffing effect of +0.038.

## Pre-committed reading

Fixed before collection.

- **Interaction ≥ +0.10 with a CI excluding zero, and the long-rung effect larger than the
  short-rung effect** → B is supported. Length is the moderator. Re-anchor the corpus to
  longer targets, re-screen at a wider margin, and item 11 proceeds, sized from the
  long-rung effect rather than from H6.
- **Interaction CI contains zero and the long-rung effect is itself null** → B is dead.
  A survives: at 4–8× the document length and with the answer up to 216 words from the
  top, moving it to the front changes nothing. Item 11's ranked table is not obtainable in
  Tier 1, and the finding to publish is the scope boundary — presentation tactics are a
  Tier 2 question (ROADMAP item 9), because their causal path runs through the retrieval
  stage this design holds constant.
- **Anything else** → inconclusive; report as an interval and do not size the round.

Recorded because the second outcome ends the largest open item on the roadmap, and is the
one most likely to get explained away.

**A design tension the probe exposed, which was not anticipated.** Clean nesting requires
the answer to be present at every rung. A document that contains the answer is a strong
answer, and the inclusive engines cite strong answers almost always — gemini is at 1.00 on
every rung of both `home_radon` and `home_smokealarm`. The pilot's targets avoided this
by being *partial* answers, which is exactly what made them screenable. So the nesting
requirement and the screening requirement pull against each other, and the two questions
carrying the burial-depth contrast are the two that saturate.

**Decision: run phase 2, with the primary contrast restricted to cells unpinned in the
control arm.** Reasons, in order:

1. Saturation is concentrated in gemini and grok (3 of 9 cells usable each). The engines
   that stay usable are gpt (7 of 9) and claude (6 of 9) — together 65% of the weighted
   panel — plus deepseek (6 of 9). The probe can still speak to most weighted traffic.
2. The restriction selects on the **control arm only**, which phase 2 does not touch. It
   is the same logic the screen already applies, and it cannot be influenced by the
   outcome being measured.
3. A pinned cell was always going to be uninformative; including it would dilute the
   estimate toward zero and make a null unreadable.

The pooled-over-all-cells figures will still be reported alongside, so the effect of this
restriction is visible rather than assumed.

**Also recorded:** `finance_housingshare` control declines monotonically with target
length, 0.52 → 0.40 → 0.34, while the other two are flat. At one question this is not a
finding, but it points the same way as H7 — a longer document is not a more cited one, and
may be a less cited one.
