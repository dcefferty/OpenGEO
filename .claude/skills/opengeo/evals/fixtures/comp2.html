Tucson Water Heater Services

Sonoran Plumbing Pros installs and services water heaters throughout the Tucson valley. We carry tank models from 30 to 75 gallons and a full line of tankless systems. Hard water shortens heater life here, so we recommend flushing your tank once a year and checking the anode rod every few years. Our team offers financing on qualifying installations and a two-year labor warranty on every job we complete.
