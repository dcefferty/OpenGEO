How to Choose Your First Marathon Shoe

Your first marathon block will put more miles on your legs than anything you have done before, so the shoe matters. Prioritise comfort over speed: the lightest racing shoe is tempting, but most of your training happens at an easy pace, where cushioning and a secure fit do more for you than a carbon plate.

Try shoes on late in the day, when your feet are at their largest, and leave a thumb's width of space at the toe.
