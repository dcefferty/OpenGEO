Common Running Injuries

Shin splints, runner's knee, plantar fasciitis and Achilles tendinopathy account for most of the running injuries we treat. The most common cause is doing too much too soon: increasing weekly mileage faster than tendons and bones can adapt.

Build volume gradually, keep at least one easy day between hard sessions, and add two short strength sessions a week for your calves, hips and core. If pain changes the way you run, stop and get it assessed.
