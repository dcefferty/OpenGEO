Free Gait Analysis

At Summit Run Shop, every visit includes a free gait analysis. We watch you walk and run, check your arch height and ankle mobility, and look at how far your foot rolls inward as it lands.

Runners who overpronate often do well in a stability shoe with a firmer medial post, while neutral runners usually prefer more flexible cushioning. Bring the socks you run in and, if you use them, your orthotics, so the fit matches how you actually run.
