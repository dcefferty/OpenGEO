Aerofoot Expands Shoe Recycling Program

Runners can now drop off worn-out athletic shoes of any brand at 200 Aerofoot stores, the company said on Tuesday. Collected shoes are ground into rubber granules for playground surfaces and running tracks.

The program, which began as a pilot in three cities last year, has collected more than 400,000 pairs. Aerofoot said it plans to add drop-off bins at partner retailers next spring.
