Cloudline 4

The Cloudline 4 is our softest daily trainer yet. A full-length supercritical foam midsole returns more energy with every stride, and a rocker profile keeps you rolling forward on long runs.

The engineered mesh upper stretches where your foot flexes and stays locked down at the heel. It weighs 9.1 ounces in a men's size 9, with an 8 mm drop and a wide, stable base for easy and recovery days.
