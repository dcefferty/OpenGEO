Water Heater Replacement in Tucson

Desert Flow Plumbing has replaced water heaters across Tucson and Marana since 2011. Our licensed technicians handle tank and tankless systems from every major manufacturer.

When your water heater starts leaking, making noise, or running out of hot water too fast, it is usually time to replace it rather than repair it. Most tank heaters last eight to twelve years in our hard water.

A standard 40-gallon tank swap costs $1,450 and a tankless install costs $2,900, including haul-away, before any work begins.

Every installation includes removal of your old unit, a new expansion tank where code requires it, and a full safety check of the gas line or electrical connection. We pull the city permit for you.
