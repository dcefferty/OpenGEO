How Much Does Water Heater Replacement Cost?

Replacing a water heater is one of the more common home repairs. The final cost depends on the fuel type, tank capacity, and whether venting or gas lines need updating. Tankless units generally cost more upfront but use less energy over time. Labor typically accounts for a meaningful share of the total, and permits add a modest fee in most cities. Always get at least two written quotes before committing to an installer.
