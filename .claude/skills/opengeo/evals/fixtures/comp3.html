Tank vs. Tankless: Which Water Heater Is Right for You?

Tank water heaters store a fixed volume of hot water and are simpler and cheaper to install. Tankless heaters warm water on demand, never run out, and take up far less space, but they need adequate gas supply or electrical capacity. Families who use a lot of hot water at once may prefer a larger tank or a high-output tankless unit. Consider your household size, your fuel source, and how long you plan to stay in the home.
