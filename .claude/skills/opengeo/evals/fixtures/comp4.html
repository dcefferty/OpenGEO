Signs You Need a New Water Heater

Rusty or discolored water, rumbling noises, and puddles near the base of the tank are common warning signs. If your heater is more than ten years old and needs repeated repairs, replacement usually makes more financial sense. A sudden drop in hot water supply can also point to sediment buildup or a failing heating element. Have a licensed plumber inspect the unit before deciding, since some problems are inexpensive to fix.
